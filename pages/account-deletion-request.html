Account Deletion Request

Last updated: January 29, 2026

⚠️ Important Notice

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data, achievements, contest history, and account information will be permanently removed from our servers. Please ensure you have downloaded or saved any important information before proceeding.

1. Overview of Account Deletion

At KhiladiLive, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and associated data at any time. This process is irreversible and should only be initiated if you are certain you want to discontinue using our platform.

2. What Gets Deleted

2.1 Personal Information

Upon account deletion, the following personal information will be permanently removed:

  • User profile information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account credentials and login information
  • Personal preferences and settings
  • Notification preferences
  • Connected social media accounts

2.2 User-Generated Data

  • Contest participation history
  • Leaderboard rankings and statistics
  • Virtual badges and achievements
  • User analytics and gameplay data
  • Custom teams and squad creations
  • Saved preferences and favorites
  • Public profile information

2.3 Communication History

  • Emails and in-app notifications
  • Support tickets and correspondence
  • Feedback and suggestions submitted
  • Any messages or communications with support team

3. What Will Not Be Deleted

For legal, compliance, and platform integrity reasons, the following information may be retained:

  • Anonymized contest results for statistical purposes
  • Transaction records required for financial compliance
  • Platform security and fraud prevention logs
  • Regulatory and tax-related records
  • IP addresses and device information (for fraud prevention)
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable platform usage data

4. Before You Delete Your Account

📋 Checklist Before Deletion

  • Download or save any important contest statistics or records
  • Export any personal achievements or leaderboard rankings
  • Note down any contest IDs or team information you want to keep
  • Ensure you are not participating in any active contests
  • Settle any pending transactions or issues
  • Withdraw any outstanding credits or rewards (if applicable)
  • Review your data one last time

5. Account Deletion Request Process

5.1 How to Request Account Deletion

Follow these steps to submit a deletion request:

  • Step 1: Log in to your KhiladiLive account
  • Step 2: Go to Account Settings → Privacy & Security
  • Step 3: Scroll to "Delete Account" section
  • Step 4: Click "Request Account Deletion"
  • Step 5: Confirm your request and provide a reason (optional)
  • Step 6: Verify your identity through email confirmation
  • Step 7: Submit the request

5.2 Manual Deletion Request (Email Method)

If you cannot access your account or prefer email-based request:

Email to: privacy@khiladilive.in

Subject Line: Account Deletion Request

Required Information to Include:

  • Full Name
  • Registered Email Address
  • KhiladiLive Username
  • Account Registration Date (if known)
  • Reason for deletion (optional)
  • Confirmation that you understand the irreversible nature

5.3 Identity Verification

We require identity verification to prevent unauthorized account deletion. You may be asked to:

  • Confirm account details and personal information
  • Verify ownership via registered email address
  • Provide additional identification if needed
  • Answer security questions associated with your account

6. Deletion Processing Timeline

6.1 Confirmation Period

  • Request Submission: Immediate upon form completion
  • Email Confirmation: We send confirmation link within 24 hours
  • Confirmation Deadline: You must confirm within 7 days
  • Cancellation Window: Can cancel request up to confirmation

6.2 Actual Deletion

  • Processing Time: 30 days after email confirmation
  • Grace Period Reason: Allows for data verification and backup
  • Full Removal: Complete deletion from all systems within 30 days
  • No Recovery: Data cannot be recovered after 30 days

7. Temporary Account Deactivation Alternative

Before permanently deleting your account, consider temporarily deactivating it:

  • Deactivation: Hides profile and data from public view
  • Reactivation: You can reactivate within 30 days
  • Data Preservation: All your data remains stored
  • Simple Process: Can be done directly from settings
  • Lower Commitment: Try before permanent deletion

8. Data Portability

Before deletion, you can request a copy of your data:

  • Request data export 30 days before deletion
  • Receive data in standard formats (CSV, JSON, PDF)
  • Export includes personal data and activity records
  • Contact privacy@khiladilive.in for data export requests

9. Deletion Disputes and Support

9.1 If Deletion Doesn't Complete

If your account isn't fully deleted after 30 days:

  • Contact support team immediately
  • Provide deletion request confirmation email
  • We will investigate and expedite deletion
  • Expect resolution within 48 hours

9.2 Cannot Undo Deletion

Please note: Once your account is permanently deleted, we cannot restore it. If you change your mind after the 30-day grace period, you must create a new account from scratch.

10. Account Deletion Due to Policy Violations

10.1 Forced Account Termination

We may forcibly delete accounts for:

  • Violation of Terms of Service
  • Fraudulent activities or cheating
  • Harassment or abusive behavior
  • Multiple violations despite warnings
  • Security threats to platform or users

10.2 Forced Deletion Rights

In such cases:

  • Deletion happens immediately without warning period
  • No grace period or cancellation option
  • You will be notified via registered email
  • You may appeal through support within 30 days

11. Data Protection and Privacy

During the deletion process, we ensure:

  • Your data is encrypted during transfer
  • Secure deletion using industry-standard methods
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties
  • Compliance with GDPR and privacy regulations
  • Regular backups are securely purged

12. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I delete my account immediately?

A: There is a 30-day grace period after confirmation to prevent accidental permanent deletion. This allows you time to reconsider or retrieve any important information.

Q: Will my contest history be visible after deletion?

A: Your personal contest history and profile will be removed. However, anonymized results may remain for statistical platform purposes.

Q: Can someone else delete my account?

A: No. Deletion requires email verification and identity confirmation. Only you can authorize deletion of your account.

Q: What if I have pending payments?

A: Settle any pending transactions before requesting deletion. We cannot process deletion requests for accounts with outstanding financial obligations.

Q: Can I export my data before deletion?

A: Yes. Request a data export at least 30 days before deletion. We'll provide your data in standard formats.

Q: Is there a way to delete my account faster?

A: The 30-day deletion period is firm to ensure data security and prevent accidental deletions. We cannot expedite this process.

This account deletion policy may be updated from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via email and in-app notifications. The most current version is always available on our website.
