Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 6, 2025

1. Introduction

KhiladiLive ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our cricket analytics platform, website, and mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Account credentials (username, password)
  • Profile information (avatar, preferences)
  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)

2.2 Usage Data

  • App usage patterns and features accessed
  • Cricket match preferences and followed teams
  • Contest participation and performance data
  • Analytics and interaction data

2.3 Technical Data

  • IP address and location data
  • Browser type and version
  • Time zone settings and language preferences
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain our cricket analytics services
  • Personalize your experience with relevant cricket data
  • Process contest entries and manage leaderboards
  • Send important updates about matches and features
  • Improve our AI predictions and analytics algorithms
  • Ensure platform security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulations

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share information only in these circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Third-party companies that help us operate our platform
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale
  • Public Leaderboards: Contest rankings (usernames only, no personal data)

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • SSL encryption for data transmission
  • Secure database storage with access controls
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Employee training on data protection

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Export your data in a readable format
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from promotional communications

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your login credentials and preferences
  • Analyze platform usage and performance
  • Provide personalized cricket content
  • Improve our services and user experience

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

8. Children's Privacy

KhiladiLive is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data during such transfers, including standard contractual clauses and adequacy decisions.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill legal obligations. Account data is typically retained for the duration of your active account, plus up to 3 years for legal and business purposes.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by email or through our platform. Your continued use of KhiladiLive after such modifications constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
