Refund Policy

Last updated: December 6, 2025

🆓 Free Platform Notice

KhiladiLive is a completely free-to-play cricket analytics platform. Since all our core features are provided at no cost, this refund policy primarily addresses future premium services and specific scenarios.

1. Overview of Our Free Services

Currently, KhiladiLive offers the following services at no charge:

  • Live cricket scores and ball-by-ball updates
  • Advanced cricket analytics and statistics
  • AI-powered match predictions and insights
  • Free-to-play social contests and leagues
  • Player performance analysis and trends
  • Community features and leaderboards
  • Mobile app access across all platforms

2. No-Cost, No-Refund Principle

Since KhiladiLive operates as a skill-based, free-to-play platform with no monetary entry fees, contest fees, or subscription charges, there are typically no financial transactions that would require refunds. Users enjoy all platform benefits without any payment obligations.

3. Future Premium Services Policy

3.1 Potential Premium Features

Should we introduce premium features in the future, they may include:

  • Advanced analytics dashboards and reports
  • Premium AI insights and detailed predictions
  • Enhanced customization and personalization options
  • Priority customer support
  • Early access to new features and beta testing
  • Advanced export and sharing capabilities

3.2 Premium Service Refund Terms

For any future paid premium services, the following refund policy will apply:

  • 7-Day Trial Period: Full refund available within 7 days of purchase
  • Service Issues: Refunds for technical problems affecting premium features
  • Billing Errors: Full refund for incorrect charges or unauthorized transactions
  • Duplicate Purchases: Refund for accidental duplicate subscriptions

4. Digital Goods and Virtual Rewards

4.1 Virtual Achievements

KhiladiLive rewards users with virtual achievements such as:

  • Badges for contest performance and milestones
  • Leaderboard rankings and recognition
  • Skill-based trophies and accomplishments
  • Community status and reputation points

These virtual rewards have no monetary value and cannot be refunded, exchanged, or transferred for real-world benefits.

4.2 Contest Participation

Since all contests are free-to-enter and skill-based, there are no entry fees to refund. Contest results are final, and virtual rewards earned through legitimate gameplay are permanent.

5. Account-Related Refund Scenarios

5.1 Account Security Issues

In cases of account security breaches:

  • Immediate investigation of any unauthorized premium purchases
  • Full refund for transactions made by unauthorized users
  • Account restoration to pre-incident state when possible
  • Enhanced security measures for affected accounts

5.2 Technical Service Failures

For significant platform outages affecting premium services:

  • Service credit or refund for extended downtime
  • Compensation for lost premium features during outages
  • Extension of premium subscription periods for technical issues
  • Alternative access methods when available

6. Third-Party Payment Processing

6.1 Payment Processor Policies

For any future premium services, payments will be processed through established providers:

  • Google Play Store for Android app purchases
  • Apple App Store for iOS app purchases
  • Secure web payment gateways for web subscriptions
  • Each platform's refund policies may also apply

6.2 Platform-Specific Refund Procedures

  • Google Play: Refunds through Google Play Store refund system
  • Apple App Store: Refunds through Apple's standard procedures
  • Direct Web Payments: Contact our support team directly
  • Processing Time: 5-10 business days for approved refunds

7. Refund Request Process

📝 How to Request a Refund

For any future premium service refunds, follow these steps to ensure prompt processing.

7.1 Required Information

When requesting a refund, please provide:

  • Account username and registered email address
  • Transaction ID or purchase confirmation number
  • Date and amount of the transaction
  • Reason for refund request
  • Supporting documentation (screenshots, error messages, etc.)

7.2 Contact Methods

  • Email: refunds@khiladilive.in
  • Support Portal: Through in-app support system
  • Response Time: 24-48 hours for initial response
  • Resolution Time: 3-5 business days for investigation

8. Non-Refundable Situations

8.1 Policy Violations

Refunds will not be provided in cases of:

  • Account termination due to Terms of Service violations
  • Cheating, fraud, or manipulation of platform features
  • Abuse of refund system or multiple fraudulent requests
  • Use of unauthorized tools or third-party software

8.2 Service Completion

  • Premium features fully utilized during subscription period
  • Digital content that has been successfully downloaded
  • Services provided as described without technical issues
  • Refund requests made after 30 days from purchase

9. Dispute Resolution

9.1 Internal Review Process

If your refund request is initially denied:

  • Request escalation to senior support team
  • Provide additional evidence or documentation
  • Allow 5-7 business days for escalated review
  • Receive detailed explanation of final decision

9.2 External Dispute Options

  • Contact your payment provider's dispute resolution service
  • File complaint with relevant consumer protection agencies
  • Seek mediation through recognized dispute resolution platforms
  • Legal action as a last resort for significant amounts

10. International Considerations

For international users:

  • Refunds processed in original payment currency
  • Exchange rate fluctuations may affect final refund amount
  • Local consumer protection laws may provide additional rights
  • Processing times may vary by country and payment method

11. Policy Updates and Changes

This refund policy may be updated to reflect:

  • Introduction of new premium services or features
  • Changes in payment processing systems
  • Updates to comply with local and international regulations
  • Improvements to customer service procedures

Users will be notified of significant policy changes via email and in-app notifications.

  • Billing Support: billing@khiladilive.in
  • General Support: support@khiladilive.in
